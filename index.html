Good to know

Before you book
How long can I leave my things with you?
Until the end of February of the following season. If you need longer, tell us when you drop off and we'll arrange it.
What can I store?
Skis, snowboards, boots, poles, paragliders and luggage up to 80 × 55 × 30 cm. Anything else, send us a photo on WhatsApp and we'll quote it.
Can you collect from my chalet or apartment?
Yes. Door-to-door pick-up or drop-off is CHF 50.– and covers collection from your address in Verbier.
What if I need my gear mid-season?
Every extra drop-off or pick-up is CHF 25.–, so you can take things out and bring them back whenever you need.
How do I book?
Message us on WhatsApp with what you'd like to store and when you're leaving. We'll confirm the price and a drop-off time.

Leaving soon?

Tell us what you want to store and when you're going. We'll send you a price the same day.
